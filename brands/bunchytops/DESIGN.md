## Do's and Don'ts

- **Don't** use the word "hustle" in copy.
- **Don't** use the word "grind" in copy.
- **Don't** use the word "epic" in copy.
- **Don't** use the word "banger" in copy.
- **Don't** use the word "stream now" in copy.
- **Don't** use the word "vibes (as a noun in copy)" in copy.
- **Don't** use the word "paradise" in copy.
- **Don't** use the word "aloha spirit" in copy.
- **Don't** use the word "journey" in copy.
- **Don't** use the word "elevate" in copy.
- **Don't** pure #000 or #fff anywhere (washed-out means lifted blacks and bloomed (not clipped) whites; haze is the brand).
- **Don't** saturated tropical cyan/teal/hibiscus palettes (tourist Hawaii — the band is local surf-indie culture, not a resort).
- **Don't** dark moody nocturne as the page's base register (rejected at hero-lock; the rebrand lives in bleached daylight — dark exists only as the single Washed Ocean anchor section).
- **Don't** hype language, urgency, fire emoji energy (86-100 BPM music; desperate marketing breaks the easy trust).
- **Don't** snappy sub-300ms entrance animations (the motion register is behind-the-beat; quick pops read as SaaS).
- **Don't** centered-everything template layout (editorial low-left/asymmetric composition is the film-still signature (locked)).
- **Don't** fully saturated color anywhere (every hue passes through the wash — ~20% desaturated, sun-faded; saturation reads as digital, not film).
